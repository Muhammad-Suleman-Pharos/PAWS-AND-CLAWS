# Supporting proposal corrections — Part 3

## Website purpose
Paws & Claws Animal Rescue is a student demonstration website for showcasing rescue programmes, adoption enquiries, volunteer opportunities and public contact. Organisational statistics and contact details from the Part 2 project are unverified and must not be represented as confirmed facts.

## Audience and goals
Audience: prospective adopters, volunteers, foster carers and community supporters. Goals: communicate rescue services, make information easy to find, enable enquiries and provide accessible navigation on mobile and desktop.

## Visual sitemap
See `../site-map.svg`. Main pages: Home → About, Services & Adoption, Enquiry, Contact. Thank-you is a supporting confirmation page.

## Delivery and risk notes
Forms require successful Netlify deployment and end-to-end testing; do not claim real email delivery from a local file. The image URLs need an internet connection; local SVG fallbacks are supplied. Obtain consent before publishing real animal details or contact information.
